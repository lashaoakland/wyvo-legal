Privacy Policy for Wyvo

Effective date: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lasha Mukhadgverdeli ("Wyvo," "we," "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Wyvo mobile app (the "App"). By using the App you agree to this Policy.

Who this is for

Wyvo is a private social app for planning and sharing activities with friends. You must be 18 or older to use Wyvo. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account: email address and password (used to sign you in).
  • Profile: display name, date of birth (to verify you are 18+), and — if you choose to add them — gender, a profile photo, bio, home city, or link.
  • Music profile (optional): the instruments you play, songs you can play or sing (title and artist only — never lyrics or chords), and whether friends can ping you for music nights.
  • Content you create: trips/plans (including music nights and who plays what), posts (short text and/or up to five photos), group and direct messages, photos you upload (chat, profile album, posts, stories), likes and reactions, polls, ideas, follows, and your "free today" status.
  • Music you attach: if you add a background song to a post, story or profile photo, we store a link to that track on Audius plus its title, artist and your chosen start point. We do not host or copy the audio.
  • Moderation: reports you file and users you block.
  • Support: the contents of messages you send us.

Information collected automatically

  • Location: with your permission, we use your device's location while the App is open to show you nearby plans and nearby public events. Your coordinates are sent to our backend to calculate distances to plans, and — when you open the Events section — to Ticketmaster's public event API to look up events near you (see "Service providers" below). Your coordinates are not shared with other users and are not stored as a location history. You can deny or revoke location access in your device settings (the App still works; plans just won't be sorted by distance and nearby events won't be shown).
  • Basic technical data needed to operate the App (e.g. authentication tokens, device/session information).

We do not collect payment information, and Wyvo contains no advertising and no third-party ad tracking.

How we use information

  • To create and manage your account and profile.
  • To provide the App's features (plans, posts, chat, photos, stories, follows, ideas, availability).
  • To sort trips by distance from your location, and to show public events happening near you.
  • To keep the community safe (process reports and blocks, enforce our Terms).
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

How information is shared

  • With other users: your profile, the content you post, and who you are in shared plans/chats are visible to other users according to each item's visibility settings (e.g. a plan can be public, followers-only, or invite-only; a story can be public or followers-only). Direct messages are visible to you and the recipient.
  • Service providers who host and run the App on our behalf:
    • Supabase — database, file storage, authentication, and realtime messaging (our backend). See supabase.com/privacy.
    • Expo — app delivery and updates. See expo.dev/privacy.
    • Audius — when you search for background music, your search query is sent to Audius's public API to return tracks, and playing an attached track streams it from Audius. See audius.co.
    • Ticketmaster — when you open the Events section, your coordinates (and any category/date filter you choose) are sent to Ticketmaster's public Discovery API to return public events near you. We do not send them your name, email, or account details. See ticketmaster.com/privacy.
    • Unsplash — stock photos on the ideas board and some plan banners come from Unsplash. To find a photo for an idea, its title is sent to Unsplash's API, and your device loads the pictures from Unsplash, which (like any website) sees your device's IP address. We do not send them your name, email, or account details. See unsplash.com/privacy.
  • Legal / safety: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users or the App.

Data retention and deletion

  • We keep your information while your account is active.
  • You can delete your account at any time from Account → Edit profile → Delete Account. This removes your profile and your content from our active systems. Backups and legally required records may persist for a limited period.
  • Stories auto-expire after 24 hours and are deleted automatically.
  • When you delete a photo from your profile album, the photo file is deleted too.
  • Photos you share in a trip chat are cleaned up after 30 days unless they have been saved into that trip's shared photo set.
  • Some shared content (e.g. messages in a group you were part of) may remain visible to that group.

Security

Access to your data is protected by authentication and per-user database access rules, and files are stored in private storage accessible only via short-lived signed links. On your phone, your sign-in is kept in the device's secure Keychain. If you turn on App Lock, Face ID / Touch ID is checked by your device itself — we never receive your face, fingerprint, or any biometric data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can exercise most of these in-app (edit profile, delete account) or by contacting us at the email below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Children

Wyvo is not directed to children. You must be 18 or older to use it. If we learn we have collected information from someone under 18, we will delete it.

International users

Your information may be processed in the United States and other countries where we or our service providers operate, which may have different data protection laws than your country.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post the new version with an updated effective date and, where appropriate, notify you in the App.
