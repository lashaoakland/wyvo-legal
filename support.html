Wyvo Support

Need help? Email us at Goservicesgroup@outlook.com and we'll get back to you within a day or two. Please send it from the email address you use for Wyvo, so we can find your account.

Common questions

I didn't get my sign-up code.

Check your spam or junk folder. On the code screen, tap Resend to get a new code — only the newest one works.

I forgot my password.

On the login screen, tap Forgot password?. We'll email you a code, and you can choose a new password in the app.

How do I report someone or something?

Tap Report on a post, reply, photo, story, plan or idea — or open the person's profile and tap Report. Our team reviews every report within 24 hours and removes content that breaks our Terms of Service.

How do I block someone?

Open their profile and tap Block, or choose Block right after you report them. You won't see their posts, plans, ideas or stories, and you can't message each other. Open their profile again to unblock.

Who can see my plans?

You choose when you post one: Everyone, Followers only, or Invite only (only the people you pick).

How do I lock Wyvo with Face ID?

Go to Profile → Edit profile → Security and turn on App Lock.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Edit profile → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, photos, messages and everything else.

Who can use Wyvo

Wyvo is for people 18 and older.
